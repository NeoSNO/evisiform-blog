Walk into almost any office, site office, clinic or warehouse and you'll still find it: a clipboard at reception with a pen on a string and a paper sign-in sheet.

It does the job, just barely. But paper sign-in sheets cause more problems than most businesses realise.

## The problem with paper sign-in sheets

- **They're hard to read.** Rushed handwriting means you often can't tell who actually visited.
- **They're not private.** Every visitor can see the names and details of everyone who signed in before them.
- **People forget to sign out.** In an emergency, you need to know who's still on site, and a paper sheet can't tell you reliably.
- **Records get lost.** Sheets get filed, binned or misplaced, right when you need to look something up.

## Why not a visitor kiosk?

Visitor management kiosks solve some of these problems, but they come with hardware to buy, tablets to mount, and often a cost for every location. For many small and medium businesses, that's hard to justify just to replace a clipboard.

## The simpler way: one QR code

With **eVisiform**, visitor sign-in comes down to a single QR code:

1. **Display your QR code** at reception or at the entrance.
2. **Visitors scan it with their own phone.** There's no app to download.
3. **They check in** by filling in a quick form on their phone.
4. **They check out** the same way when they leave.

No kiosk, no tablet, no extra hardware, and no per-location cost, so it's easy to roll out across every site you manage.

## Why it works

Visitors already have a phone in their pocket, and scanning a QR code takes seconds. You get clear, digital sign-in records instead of a pile of handwritten pages, and each visitor's details stay private from the next person in line.
